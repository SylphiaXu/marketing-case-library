# Marketing Case Library

**What worked, what the evidence supports, and what I would apply next.**

A collection of marketing and growth case analyses across consumer brands, product marketing, and AI go-to-market.

这是我的营销案例研究库。重点是理解受众、策略、渠道与结果之间的关系，并写出自己的判断和可迁移原则。

## Research process

Collect → verify dates and sources → select → analyze → review → index.

For each case, distinguish the original launch date, reporting date, and any later development. Prioritize original brand announcements and inspectable evidence. Label brand-reported outcomes, third-party estimates, and my own inferences separately.

## Quality standard

Each completed case states the business goal, audience insight, strategy, channels, evidence, limitations, transferable principles, and my own judgment. If outcomes are not public, write “not publicly disclosed.” Include an AI angle only when it adds something useful.

## Planned presentation

Build the written collection first. A searchable HTML collection and a small selection of presentation-ready cases come later, after enough reviewed content exists. They are not implemented yet.
